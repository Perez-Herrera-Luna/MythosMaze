# MythosMaze

Mythos Maze is a first-person 3D roguelike game developed in Unity. You play as a foolish mortal of Greek legend who has challenged the gods for immortality. The gods are willing to grant your request if you can best the Minotaur's maze. Players navigate the maze in search of its center whilst battling the myriad of mythical creatures along the way. The maze is procedurally generated on every playthrough using a variation of a random walk algorithm. Mythos Maze is compatible with Windows 8.1 and newer operating systems.
