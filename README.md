# MythosMaze

CS Capstone Project

In Mythos Maze, you play as a greek civilian who has challenged the gods, requesting to prove yourself worthy of immortality. Amused, the gods accept your challenge, declaring that if you can survive the minotaur's maze, they shall grant you immortality. Do you have what it takes to navigate the maze, survive the mythical creatures inhabiting it, and defeat the minotaur?

Mythos Maze is a first-person 3D roguelike game developed in Unity. It features an assortment of rooms/enemy arenas procedurally generate throughout the world, using a variation of the drunkard's walk algorithm to form pathways connecting them. It is compatible with Windows 8.1 and newer operating systems.
